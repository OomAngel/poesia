They took the work I had given everything to.
There was no place for what I brought. No reciprocity.

But the ground does not stop turning. Not because the winter deserves it —
but because that is what ground does.

In the years when there was nothing, the green returned.
In the years of confusion, it returned.
It will return now too.

It is a covenant. Not with the company, not with the market.
With something older than any of that.
