My profile is a collage. I go wide when others go deep.
I can frame a problem in ways others cannot imagine —
but which of those versions is me?

Interested in everything. Hard to integrate into a single identity.
The documented trait, stable: extreme openness.
It is not a flaw to fix. It is a way of being in the world.
