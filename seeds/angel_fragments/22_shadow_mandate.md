Responsibility without authority. To see the risk, unable to act.
To identify the flaw, have no one adopt the correction.
The one who carries the weight without a voice in the decision.

It is not complaint — it is the geometry of a place that does not serve.
Where hierarchy protects those who do not reason.
Where initiative dies without credit.
