I build in a language that is not my first.
Every word chosen with care — because I cannot rely on instinct.
This makes me slower. It also makes me more precise.

To create in an adopted tongue is to live in translation.
You learn that nothing maps exactly.
And that the gaps between languages hold more than the words.
