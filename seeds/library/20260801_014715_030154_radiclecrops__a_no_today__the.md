The radicle that splits the sleeping seed
reaches for light through soil and stubborn clay;
quinoa is the root the world will need,
a harvest hope for a diminished day.
I met the hands that hold this gentle trust,
and loved the quiet force of what you mean;
today the door said no — but doorways must
give way to those who learn what might have been.
I take your mission like a seed to sow
in ground I'll train myself to understand;
what one door closes, other doors will know,
when my own roots have learned to meet the land.
And when my path has crossed a thousand ways,
may it converge with yours in greener days.
