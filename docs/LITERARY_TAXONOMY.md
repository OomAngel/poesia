# Literary Taxonomy

_Reference document for movements, eras, and schools. Implementation deferred to Phase 4._

## Spanish-Language Movements

| Movement | Era | Characteristics | Representative Poets |
|----------|-----|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Siglo de Oro** | 1500-1680 | Baroque complexity, conceptismo, culteranismo, religious themes | Góngora, Quevedo, Sor Juana, Lope de Vega |
| **Romanticismo** | 1830-1860 | Emotion, nature, nationalism, melancholy, liberty | Bécquer, Espronceda, Acuña |
| **Modernismo** | 1880-1920 | Musicality, exoticism, formal elegance, synesthesia, French influence | Rubén Darío, Gutiérrez Nájera, Urbina, Nervo |
| **Generación del 98** | 1898-1920 | Spanish identity crisis, austerity, landscape as interior, existential | Antonio Machado, Unamuno, Juan Ramón Jiménez |
| **Generación del 27** | 1920-1936 | Surrealism + tradition, metaphor, popular + cultured, vanguardia | García Lorca, Alberti, Cernuda, Hernández |
| **Poesía Social** | 1940-1960 | Political engagement, accessibility, witness | Blas de Otero, Gabriel Celaya |
| **Contemporáneos (MX)** | 1920-1940 | Pure poetry, cosmopolitanism, intellectual rigor | Xavier Villaurrutia, López Velarde |

## English-Language Movements

| Movement | Era | Characteristics | Representative Poets |
|----------|-----|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Romanticism** | 1785-1830 | Nature, imagination, emotion, individual, sublime | Wordsworth, Keats, Shelley, Blake |
| **Victorian** | 1837-1901 | Moral seriousness, narrative, doubt, empire | Tennyson, Browning, Hardy, Mackay |
| **Georgian** | 1910-1936 | Rural England, pastoral, accessible, traditional forms | Housman, de la Mare, Brooke |
| **Imagism** | 1912-1917 | Precision, concrete image, free verse, economy | Pound, H.D., Williams |
| **American Transcendentalism** | 1830-1860 | Nature as spiritual, individualism, democratic | Whitman, Emerson |
| **American Modernism** | 1900-1940 | Experimentation, fragmentation, free verse | Frost, Dickinson (proto), Stevens |

## Dutch Movements

| Movement | Era | Characteristics | Representative Poets |
|----------|-----|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Tachtigers** | 1880-1890 | "Art for art's sake", individualism, sensory beauty, anti-bourgeois | Willem Kloos, Albert Verwey |
| **Symbolisme (NL)** | 1890-1910 | Suggestion, musicality, mystery | P.C. Boutens |
| **Expressionisme (NL)** | 1910-1930 | Cosmic vision, vitalism, dynamic imagery | Hendrik Marsman |
| **Forum-generatie** | 1930-1940 | Personal authenticity, accessible, "vent" (fellow) poetry | J.C. Bloem, Slauerhoff |

## Tonal Qualities by Movement

| Movement | Typical Tones |
|----------|---------------|
| Siglo de Oro | ornate, intellectual, paradoxical, devotional |
| Romanticismo | melancholic, passionate, nostalgic, defiant |
| Modernismo | musical, sensual, exotic, elegant, wistful |
| Gen. 98 | spare, meditative, honest, austere, landscape-interior |
| Gen. 27 | surreal, popular, duende, tragic, luminous |
| Romanticism (EN) | sublime, yearning, imaginative, solitary |
| Victorian | earnest, doubting, narrative, moral |
| Imagism | precise, visual, restrained, concrete |
| Tachtigers | sensory, individual, beautiful, rebellious |

---

_This taxonomy is a navigation aid, not a constraint. Poets cross boundaries._
