# Influence Registry

_Angel's poetic influences. Minimal profiles now; richer profiles in Phase 4._

## Spanish / Latin American

### Antonio Machado (1875-1939)
- **Movement:** Generación del 98
- **Tone:** spare, meditative, honest, austere, landscape-as-interior
- **Forms:** romance, soneto, free verse
- **Exemplars:** "Caminante, son tus huellas el camino y nada más" · "Hoy es siempre todavía"

### Pablo Neruda (1904-1973)
- **Movement:** Surrealism → Political → Late simplicity
- **Tone:** sensual, elemental, political, expansive, intimate
- **Exemplars:** "Puedo escribir los versos más tristes esta noche" · "Me gustas cuando callas"

### Luis G. Urbina (1864-1934)
- **Movement:** Modernismo (Mexican)
- **Tone:** melancholic, tender, intimate, nostalgic
- **Exemplars:** "Vieja lágrima"

### Manuel Gutiérrez Nájera (1859-1895)
- **Movement:** Modernismo (precursor)
- **Tone:** elegant, musical, wistful, Parisian
- **Exemplars:** "Quiero morir cuando decline el día"

### Miguel Hernández (1910-1942)
- **Movement:** Generación del 27
- **Tone:** earthy, passionate, tragic, popular
- **Exemplars:** "Llegó con tres heridas: la del amor, la de la muerte, la de la vida"

### Rubén Darío (1867-1916)
- **Movement:** Modernismo (founder)
- **Tone:** musical, exotic, formal, sensual, cosmopolitan
- **Exemplars:** "Juventud, divino tesoro, ¡ya te vas para no volver!"

### Octavio Paz (1914-1998)
- **Movement:** Surrealism, Contemporáneos
- **Tone:** intellectual, erotic, philosophical, luminous
- **Exemplars:** "Entre lo que veo y digo, entre lo que digo y callo"

### Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz (1648-1695)
- **Movement:** Baroque / Siglo de Oro
- **Tone:** intellectual, witty, feminist, paradoxical
- **Exemplars:** "Hombres necios que acusáis a la mujer sin razón"

### Gustavo Adolfo Bécquer (1836-1870)
- **Movement:** Post-Romanticismo
- **Tone:** intimate, musical, delicate, melancholic
- **Exemplars:** "¿Qué es poesía? ¿Y tú me lo preguntas? Poesía... eres tú."

### Manuel Acuña (1849-1873)
- **Movement:** Romanticismo (Mexican)
- **Tone:** passionate, tragic, youthful, melancholic
- **Exemplars:** "Nocturno a Rosario"

### Federico García Lorca (1898-1936)
- **Movement:** Generación del 27
- **Tone:** duende, popular, tragic, surreal, musical
- **Exemplars:** "Verde que te quiero verde" · "A las cinco de la tarde"

### Gabriela Mistral (1889-1957)
- **Movement:** Post-Modernismo
- **Tone:** maternal, tender, biblical, earthy, sorrowful
- **Exemplars:** "Piececitos de niño, azulosos de frío"

### Ramón López Velarde (1888-1921)
- **Movement:** Post-Modernismo / Contemporáneos precursor
- **Tone:** provincial, erotic, Catholic guilt, nostalgic
- **Exemplars:** "Mi prima Águeda" · "Suave Patria"

## English / American

### Charles Mackay (1814-1889)
- **Movement:** Victorian popular verse
- **Tone:** moral, accessible, anthemic
- **Exemplars:** "You have no enemies, you say?"

### Thomas Hardy (1840-1928)
- **Movement:** Victorian / Early Modern
- **Tone:** pessimistic, ironic, rural, fatalistic
- **Exemplars:** "Woman much missed, how you call to me"

### A.E. Housman (1859-1936)
- **Movement:** Georgian / Late Victorian
- **Tone:** elegiac, stoic, pastoral, death-haunted
- **Exemplars:** "Into my heart an air that kills" · "To an Athlete Dying Young"

### William Wordsworth (1770-1850)
- **Movement:** Romanticism
- **Tone:** contemplative, nature-mystical, democratic, nostalgic
- **Exemplars:** "I wandered lonely as a cloud"

### John Keats (1795-1821)
- **Movement:** Romanticism
- **Tone:** sensuous, mortal, beautiful, melancholic
- **Exemplars:** "A thing of beauty is a joy forever" · "Ode to a Nightingale"

### Robert Frost (1874-1963)
- **Movement:** American Modernism (traditional forms)
- **Tone:** rural, deceptively simple, dark undertow, conversational
- **Exemplars:** "Two roads diverged in a yellow wood" · "Good fences make good neighbors"

### Walt Whitman (1819-1892)
- **Movement:** American Transcendentalism
- **Tone:** expansive, democratic, erotic, cosmic, cataloguing
- **Exemplars:** "I contain multitudes" · "O Captain! My Captain!"

### Emily Dickinson (1830-1886)
- **Movement:** American proto-Modernist
- **Tone:** compressed, slant, death-intimate, surprising
- **Exemplars:** "Because I could not stop for Death" · "Tell all the truth but tell it slant"

## Dutch

### Willem Kloos (1859-1938)
- **Movement:** Tachtigers
- **Tone:** individualist, sensory, rebellious, "art for art's sake"
- **Exemplars:** "Ik ben een God in 't diepst van mijn gedachten"

### J.C. Bloem (1887-1966)
- **Movement:** Forum-generatie
- **Tone:** melancholic, autumnal, resigned, accessible
- **Exemplars:** "Voorbij, voorbij, o en voorgoed voorbij"

### Hendrik Marsman (1899-1940)
- **Movement:** Expressionisme / Vitalism
- **Tone:** cosmic, dynamic, vital, patriotic
- **Exemplars:** "Denkend aan Holland zie ik brede rivieren"
